THE IDEA

01 / 03

For the life we share,
and the questions we don’t.

Church of Life is a personal thought experiment: an imagined approach to building a better world for everyone. It starts with a simple idea: life matters, and so does how we treat one another.

We don’t have to agree on everything. We can make room for different beliefs, seek understanding together, and choose not to harm others along the way.

A FOUNDATION FOR THE FUTURE

Four tenets. One shared world.

Ideas to guide how we understand existence, live alongside one another, and care for life.

01 / THE ULTIMATE QUEST✳

Stay curious
about existence.

Explore the cosmos, question the nature of reality, and seek to understand suffering. Wonder is welcome, but no one is obligated to make it their life’s work.

THE ULTIMATE QUEST
02 / UNITY & DIVERSITY◌

Many perspectives.
One humanity.

We share an interest in improving life for everyone. Our differences can enrich our collective inquiry rather than divide us.

UNITY AND DIVERSITY
03 / TRUTH & BELIEF✧

Hold beliefs
with openness.

Consider spiritual, philosophical, and scientific perspectives with reason and evidence. No belief is beyond scrutiny, and no understanding beyond revision.

TRUTH AND BELIEF
04 / MORALITY♡

Choose care.
Reduce suffering.

The experiences of conscious beings matter. Act with kindness and respect, support the freedom and well-being of others, and alleviate suffering where possible.

MORALITY
✳

“People do not need to agree on everything. People do need to respect that there are different opinions.”
